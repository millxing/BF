PSB Staffing Numbers: Comparing Budget and DESE Reports

A short guide to the Brookline Public Schools staffing numbers that come up most often in budget discussions.

Several Brookline staffing figures circulate in budget conversations, and they can look contradictory at first glance. Most of the confusion comes from mixing reports that count different things.

DESE district-profile reports use narrower categories such as total enrollment, educator FTE, or teachers by program area. Brookline’s FY27 school budget uses a broader staffing chart that counts all staff regardless of funding source.

That is why DESE can show about 54 special education teachers while the PSB budget shows 386.1 FTE in its broader Special Education category. The same caution applies to claims about administrators: the FY27 budget’s 216.4 district-personnel figure is a district office staffing total, not a count of administrators alone.
